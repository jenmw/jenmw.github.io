About me

At Breakroom, a job website for frontline workers, I worked end-to-end across the whole product in a small cross-functional team, designing for both sides of the marketplace — job-seekers and employers. Pragmatic and user-centric, I served as custodian of the Breakroom brand, ran experiments in ambiguity to shape the direction of future design work, and utilised AI across all phases of design, from prototyping and research to content design and iteration.

In EdTech, I was the design lead embedded across multiple product teams, stepping in for print-based teams that had no digital designers. I navigated hard conversations about scope and quality to deliver digital games in often short timescales, always working within their team's brand voice.

Mentored by a leader in GDS design principles, I do the hard work to make things simple and design openly and inclusively. I'm looking for teams who care about content, outcomes, and the real people on the other side of the screen.
